# 30-Assembly-Instructions

The site assembly guide for two people with a cordless driver (5 mm hex bit) and the kit's 5 mm hex key:

- **[100000_P04_POD-INSTALL-SHEET_INS.md](100000_P04_POD-INSTALL-SHEET_INS.md)** (DRAFT): what the room needs (IF-17): clear height, floor, the 120 V outlet, the air's clear space, getting the pallets in.
- **[100000_P04_POD-ASSEMBLY-GUIDE_INS.md](100000_P04_POD-ASSEMBLY-GUIDE_INS.md)** (DRAFT): 22 numbered steps with parts, fasteners, who holds what, time per step, safety notes and the final check.

Both are generated by `v2/07-Packaging/pack.py` from `v2/07-Packaging/templates/` (`assembly-guide.md`, `install-sheet.md`), from the same list of pieces as the packing plan, so labels, steps, bolt counts and times always agree. Edit the template or the script, never the guide itself. Each step's picture is drawn from the parts' own CAD (below).

**Step pictures.** `step_drawings.py` draws one picture per step that fits parts, outside Fusion, as an exact line drawing
(the drawing generator's hidden-line projection) of every piece fitted so far, from its CAD on main (`pack.py`'s CAD map
and its rules). The step's pieces are black with a balloon holding each packing label, the pieces fitted before grey; a
piece with no CAD yet is drawn dashed from its skeleton P04 block and marked CAD TO COME. Each step has its own camera
(and cut-away) and arrows where a move's direction matters; a part that goes into another is drawn clear of it. The
step's fixings, as the guide counts them, are listed under the picture. Each picture's PDF is read back before its PNG
is written (every balloon holds one of the step's labels, every label has its balloon, each leader ends on its own
part), and the set redraws byte for byte the same. It needs the drawing generator's own Python (`tools/drawgen/run.sh`
makes it):

```sh
DG=$HOME/.cache/spaceship-drawgen/venv-ocp7.8.1-mupdf1.27.2/bin/python
$DG v2/02-Drawings/30-Assembly-Instructions/step_drawings.py           # every step's picture into img/
$DG v2/02-Drawings/30-Assembly-Instructions/step_drawings.py --check   # are the committed pictures current?
$DG v2/02-Drawings/30-Assembly-Instructions/test_step_drawings.py      # its tests
python3 v2/07-Packaging/pack.py                                         # puts each picture under its step
```

Redraw the set whenever a part's CAD, the pieces or the steps change; check K12 keeps every skeleton block the
pictures use mapped to its label.
