## 350001 DL, 350002 DT, 350003 DR: decor blocks, stage 1 (shells and fitted parts), P01

The three blocks keep the skeleton's measured v1 shapes exactly (D-01, D-49): the same plan and front outlines, facets, the DT/DR joint on v1's line to X 449, the slope DT shares with DL and the top chamfer. Each block is a 3 mm ACP skin (EV3038 Dark Yellow, D-12) made by shelling that shape inward, left open where the blocks meet each other and at the back, where a back plate closes it. The aluminium frames inside the skins (hidden: 6063-T6 mill finish, D-79) are stage 2, so no hole or fixing is placed yet: each fixing below is marked for stage 2.
