## 350001 DL, 350002 DT, 350003 DR: decor blocks, P01 (stage 2: tube frames)

The three blocks keep the skeleton's measured v1 shapes exactly (D-01, D-49): the same plan and front outlines, facets, the DT/DR joint on v1's line to X 449, the slope DT shares with DL and the top chamfer. Each block is a 3 mm ACP skin (EV3038 Dark Yellow, D-12) made by shelling that shape inward, left open where the blocks meet each other and at the back, and bonded to a welded frame of 6063-T6 tube (hidden, mill finish, D-79), as v1's frames of 16 mm steel box (v1 decor frame drawing): 20 × 20 × 1.5 tube has about the same bending stiffness (E·I 4.5e8 against 4.7e8 N·mm²) at 0.30 kg/m against 0.47. Every tube corner is an expression of the skeleton and the block's own parameters (fully constrained sketches); each tube is shelled to its real wall with its ends open. The frames were checked offline before the build: no tube overlaps another, the air parts, the hinge pockets, the panels or the glass channels, and every tube corner lies inside the skin.
