## H. Calculations

Each line names its inputs and their status. The layout study (`v2/05-Engineering/layout-study/`) and the sound and air check (`v2/05-Engineering/acoustics-air/`) replace these first estimates with their own scripts.

- **C1 Conversions (calculation).** 1 in = 25.4 mm exactly. 48 × 40 in = 1219.2 × 1016.0 mm. 48 × 96 in = 1219.2 × 2438.4 mm. 6 ft = 1828.8 mm. 36 × 80 in = 914.4 × 2032.0 mm. Rounded to whole mm in the tables.
- **C2 CO2 at steady state (calculation).** Rise above the supply air: ΔC = n·G / (Q·ε). Inputs: n = 2 people; G = 0.0052 l/s of CO2 per seated adult (assumption, a typical value); supply air at 400 ppm, near outdoor level (assumption); mixing effectiveness ε = 1 (perfect mixing) or the PM's 0.8 (D-52). For CO2 ≤ 800 ppm, ΔC ≤ 400 ppm, so Q ≥ 2 × 0.0052 / 0.000400 = **26 l/s**. In a 600 ppm office (PLAN Target 4, D-43), ΔC ≤ 200 ppm, so Q ≥ 52 l/s with perfect mixing. That is why two people get 53 l/s. One person makes half the CO2, so 26 l/s holds 800 ppm for one person in a 600 ppm office with perfect mixing; that is the floor while someone is inside (D-44). At ε = 0.8 the same office needs 0.0052 / (0.000200 × 0.8) = **about 33 l/s** for one person and **65 l/s** for two, the boost (D-52); at the 26 l/s floor one person sits at about 850 ppm, so the CO2 control raises the flow (HW §7a). At the 53 l/s normal flow (D-33), ΔC = 0.0104 / 53 = 196 ppm, so CO2 ≈ **596 ppm** in a 400 ppm room, or **796 ppm** in a 600 ppm office (845 ppm at ε = 0.8). At the 65 l/s boost, ΔC = 160 ppm, so CO2 ≈ 760 ppm in a 600 ppm office (800 ppm at ε = 0.8). Office air is often above 400 ppm; the pod then sits higher by the same amount.
- **C3 Air change (calculation).** Inside about 2338 × 1460 × 2301 mm (REQ-BLD-06) = 7.85 m³; v1 was 2372 × 1480 × 2223 mm = 7.80 m³ (V1 BOM summary). At the 53 l/s normal flow: 7.85 / 0.053 = 148 s, **about 2.5 minutes**. At the 65 l/s boost: 121 s, about 2 minutes.
- **C4 Heat (calculation).** Temperature rise ΔT = P / (ρ · c_p · Q), with ρ = 1.2 kg/m³ and c_p = 1005 J/(kg·K) (air at about 20 °C, source: standard values). Heat inputs, all assumptions: 2 seated people at 75 W sensible each, 2 laptops at 30 W each, lights 30 W, so P = 240 W. At the 53 l/s normal flow: ΔT = 240 / (1.2 × 1005 × 0.053) = **3.8 K** (the Hardware Engineer's model gives 3.6 K). At the 65 l/s boost: **3.1 K**. At the 26 l/s floor with one person inside (75 + 30 + 30 = 135 W): 4.3 K. Two people at 26 l/s would reach 7.7 K, one more reason they get 53 l/s (D-44). D-50 holds the inside at most 4 K over the room: on these inputs that takes about 28 l/s for one person and 50 l/s for two (the Hardware Engineer's model gives 27 and 48 l/s, and 61 l/s for two with the voice-kit reserve). The voice cockpit reserve (60 W) adds 0.9 K at 53 l/s. So the flow well above the CO2 minimum, and the boost, are what keep the pod cool, which matters because heat is the users' top complaint (MS).
- **C5 Glass mass (calculation).** Door area 2.080 m² and fixed glass area 1.344 m², from the v1 STEP outlines (polygon area). Glass density 2500 kg/m³ (assumption: typical soda-lime glass), so 25 kg/m² at 10 mm and 20 kg/m² at 8 mm. Door: 52.0 kg at 10 mm, **41.6 kg at 8 mm**. Fixed glass: 33.6 kg at 10 mm, **26.9 kg at 8 mm**. Both: 85.6 kg at 10 mm, 68.5 kg at 8 mm, so 8 mm saves 17.1 kg. The v1 BOM's rectangles (3.91 m²) overstate the area by 14 % (ISS-023). Decision D-27 makes the front 4+4 laminated glass (8.76 mm): 8 mm of glass at 20.0 kg/m² plus a 0.76 mm PVB interlayer at about 0.8 kg/m² (assumption: about 1070 kg/m³), so about 20.8 kg/m². That makes the door about **43.3 kg** and the fixed panel about **28.0 kg**.
- **C6 Mass per floor area (calculation).** Footprint 2.5 × 1.7 = 4.25 m². 418 / 4.25 = **98.4 kg/m²** at PR #11's figure, and 425 / 4.25 = 100.0 kg/m² at the layout study's own list, while the weight is rechecked (D-54). v1: 480 / 4.25 = 112.9 kg/m² (PLAN). The light build at 300 kg would be 70.6 kg/m².
- **C7 Step into the pod (calculation).** v1 floor top at Z(v1) = 115 mm (V1 STEP), plus 15 mm of feet, gives about 130 mm above the room floor. Thin floor (D-05; build PROPOSED in open-questions Q4): about 20 mm.
- **C8 12 V DC load (calculation).** Voice cockpit 12 V × 5 A = 60 W (DECIDED, D-11). Lights ≤ 30 W (REQ-AIR-06, assumption). Four 12 V fans (D-51), about 30 W in all (assumption: the maker's power figure wasn't found, SEL; the fan-sample test sets it, ISS-077). Sensor hub about 5 W (assumption until its datasheet arrives, ISS-060). Total about 125 W, which is about 10.4 A at 12 V. A supply rated at 150 W or more (12.5 A) leaves about 20 % margin. Every load is also heat inside the pod (C4).
