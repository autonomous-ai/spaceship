The PM makes product and design decisions and records each one here, with its reason (`AGENTS.md`). Nothing waits for outside approval. The guideline's engineering rules still apply, but this log replaces its approval steps. There are no human hardware leads on this project (D-32): issues close on the PM's logged decision plus evidence, and the factory's wall-and-door sample is the physical check.

A decision here is **DECIDED**. Everything else in the control pack marked PROPOSED is a working answer until the PM rules on it. To change a decision, add a new row that says which one it replaces; don't change an old row's decision. Its notes on other files' status, and factual slips in its reason, may be corrected.

**Current headline (D-27, D-37, D-49 to D-53, D-59 to D-88):** a 4+4 laminated front of toughened, heat-soaked panes, frameless as in v1, over a 13 mm threshold (D-72); walls and ceiling on one 84 mm stack (D-66), joined by captive ISO 4762 M6 × 16 bolts with one 5 mm key at 2.26 mm metal-stop seams (D-63 to D-65); the pod at 415.2 kg as shipped with the savings of D-81 and D-85, D-86's glide load blocks and ELECTRICAL's parts: 4.8 kg under the 420 kg cap and 5.2 kg over the 410 kg target, with the voice kit's 4.0 kg reserve outside the cap (calculation, the layout study's live count, PR #68); about 19.3 dB calculated (range 14.6–21.7, about 24 dB at best), with the wall sample deciding (D-53); fresh air driven by CO2 and heat, holding 800 ppm in a 600 ppm office and at most 4 K over the room, never below 26 l/s while someone is inside (D-50, D-52), through a felt-covered air column and soffit inside the pod: 25.3 dB(A) at 53 l/s and 29.7 at the 65 l/s boost, calculated (D-60 to D-62); the voice kit on 40 W on average and 60 W at peak, with the fans boosting to hold 4 K at 30 dB(A) or less (D-88); the voice kit on 40 W on average and 60 W at peak, with the fans boosting to hold 4 K at 30 dB(A) or less (D-88); decor blocks 205 mm deep and side walls 1495 deep, as v1 measures (D-49).
