# Decision log: Spaceship (FocusPod v2)

| Doc code | Revision | Status | Date | Owner |

| V2-CTL-008 | P01 | Live log | 2026-10-31 | The PM (the Swarm Lead) |

The PM makes product and design decisions and records each one here, with its reason (`AGENTS.md`). Nothing waits for outside approval. The guideline's engineering rules still apply, but this log replaces its approval steps. There are no human hardware leads on this project (D-32): issues close on the PM's logged decision plus evidence, and the factory's wall-and-door sample is the physical check.

A decision here is **DECIDED**. Everything else in the control pack marked PROPOSED is a working answer until the PM rules on it. To change a decision, add a new row that says which one it replaces; don't change an old row's decision. Its notes on other files' status, and factual slips in its reason, may be corrected.

**Current headline (D-27, D-37, D-49 to D-53, D-59 to D-88, D-131 to D-137):** a 4+4 laminated front of toughened, heat-soaked panes, frameless as in v1, with no floor and no step at the door, so the drop seal closes on the room floor (D-132); walls and ceiling on one 84 mm stack (D-66) on welded galvanized steel frames with the same outside sections (D-131), joined by captive ISO 4762 M6 × 16 bolts with one 5 mm key at 2.26 mm metal-stop seams (D-63 to D-65); the pod at 409.0 kg as shipped on the merged count, which still has the aluminium frames and the floor; D-131's steel frames add about 40 to 65 kg and D-132's no floor takes off about 21 (assumption: the PM's estimates), under a cap of 460 kg as shipped, provisional until the sample panel's recount (D-131; it was 420), with the voice kit's 4.0 kg reserve outside the cap (calculation, the layout study's live count with AIR's parts from CAD (PR #91) and D-130's intake boot and gaskets (PR #254), after FRONT's decor blocks (PR #105), WALL's B1 and B2 back panels and their cassettes (PR #205), D-121's and D-124's fan boxes (PR #199) and D-117's frame cleats (PR #195); D-116's thinner casings are withdrawn (D-121)); about 19.3 dB calculated (range 14.6–21.7, about 24 dB at best), with the wall sample deciding (D-53); fresh air driven by CO2 and heat, holding 800 ppm in a 600 ppm office and at most 4 K over the room, never below 26 l/s while someone is inside (D-50, D-52), through a felt-covered air column and soffit inside the pod: 25.2 dB(A) at 53 l/s and 29.6 at the 65 l/s boost, the boost reaching 66.2 l/s at 30 dB(A), with D-130's sealed intake boot and gaskets (calculation, the air report, PR #254; DR as built without them, 33.3 and 37.8): 0.4 dB(A) under the 30 dB(A) limit, a risk until the fan sample measures it (ISS-090); the voice kit on 40 W on average and 60 W at peak, on one 24 V Class 2 feed, with the fans boosting to hold 4 K at 30 dB(A) or less (D-88, D-168); designed for US use, with the mains side US 120 V, 60 Hz on UL-listed parts, 12 V for the fans and the hub and 24 V for the lights and the kit (D-168), and certification work later (D-10, D-133, D-134); decor blocks 205 mm deep and side walls 1495 deep, as v1 measures (D-49).

The control pack wrote the reasons from the PM's messages of 2026-10-31 and from the sources named. The PM can reword them.
