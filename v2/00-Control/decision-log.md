# Decision log: Spaceship (FocusPod v2)

| Doc code | Revision | Status | Date | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V2-CTL-008 | P01 | Live log | 2026-10-31 | The PM (the Swarm Lead) |

The PM makes product and design decisions and records each one here, with its reason (`AGENTS.md`). Nothing waits for outside approval. The guideline's engineering rules still apply, but this log replaces its approval steps. The hardware engineering lead and the indoor pod lead engineer review alongside the work, and their comments come back to the PM.

A decision here is **DECIDED**. Everything else in the control pack marked PROPOSED is a working answer until the PM rules on it. To change a decision, add a new row that says which one it replaces; don't edit an old row.

The control pack wrote the reasons from the PM's messages of 2026-10-31 and from the sources named. The PM can reword them.

| ID | Date | Decision | Reason | What it affects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | 2026-10-31 | **The look stays unchanged**: the faceted front, size, proportions, colours and glass layout. Every saving is invisible. | The faceted front is the team's iconic design and the product people know (PLAN.md Goal; `AGENTS.md`). | REQ-LOOK-01…05; every outside part; ISS-013, ISS-031, ISS-044 |
| D-02 | 2026-10-31 | **300 kg stays the goal. The build lands at about 380 kg with about 22–23 dB speech reduction (ISO 23351-1 Class C), the best value. 400 kg is the hard cap.** The 22–23 dB is calculated, and the factory sample decides the real figure. | The calculated lightest wall that still reaches about 22–23 dB comes out near 380 kg. Holding 300 kg would give up too much quiet, and the cap stops the trade from creeping. For scale, the calculation gives v1 about 15 dB as built. | REQ-LGT-01, REQ-SND-01, REQ-SND-03; WALL, CEILING, FRONT; ISS-009, ISS-042, ISS-063, ISS-065. PLAN.md Targets 1 and 5 still say 300 kg and "aim 30 dB". |
| D-03 | 2026-10-31 | **Every part weighs 20 kg or less, except the glass.** | One person can carry each piece through a normal door and up stairs, with no pallet jack (PLAN.md Target 1 and scorecard). | REQ-LGT-02; panel split; ISS-047 |
| D-04 | 2026-10-31 | **Ship on 48 × 40 in pallets plus one standard long pallet. The decor blocks are not split.** | Standard LTL pallets only. The long items (door, fixed panel, side decor blocks about 2300) ride one long pallet. Not splitting the decor keeps the faceted look free of extra seams and joints. | REQ-SHP-01, REQ-SHP-02; PACKAGING; ISS-057; layout study |
| D-05 | 2026-10-31 | **Thin floor**: panels about 2 cm thick on the room floor, walls fixed around them, base band kept as trim. Replaces the build plan's raised steel floor. | About 50 kg lighter. The step drops from about 13 cm to about 2 cm and there's about 10 cm more headroom. It still closes the bottom of the box for sound (PLAN.md; open-questions.md Q4). | BASE; BP-08, BP-24, BP-30; plan VT03-ST, VT04 and VT12 dropped; ISS-040, ISS-050, ISS-053, ISS-054 |
| D-06 | 2026-10-31 | **No table.** The pod ships empty. | Customers furnish it however they like (PLAN.md). | INTERIOR; ISS-006 |
| D-07 | 2026-10-31 | **Smoked acrylic front (door and fixed panel), with laminated glass as the fallback. The factory sample decides.** | The front is the heaviest single item and the weak spot for sound. Pick the most dB per kg. Acrylic is far lighter with the same smoked look; laminated glass is quieter if acrylic fails the sample. | FRONT 360000; REQ-LOOK-04; hinge rating; ISS-002, ISS-043 |
| D-08 | 2026-10-31 | **Tool-free connectors, captive bolts, factory-made subassemblies and pre-fitted felt.** | Two people in about an hour with one tool means almost nothing gets built on site. Captive bolts can't get lost. Felt fitted at the factory removes the slowest site step. | REQ-ASM-03, REQ-ASM-05, REQ-ASM-10, REQ-ASM-11; BP-07; LINING; ISS-015, ISS-047, ISS-064 |
| D-09 | 2026-10-31 | **Fresh air at 70 l/s**, with a CO2-driven fan that starts by itself, **25 dB(A) or less** inside, using the in-house Desk Pro AI sensor hub. | Heat and stale air are the users' top complaint. 70 l/s keeps CO2 near 550 ppm and the pod within about 3 K of the room. The in-house hub already measures CO2 and presence. Nobody publishes fan noise, so a quiet published figure is a first (PLAN.md scorecard). | REQ-AIR-01…07, REQ-ELE-02, REQ-ELE-04; AIR, ELECTRICAL; ISS-038, ISS-039, ISS-046 |
| D-10 | 2026-10-31 | **A 12 V DC bus feeds the fans, lights, sensors and voice kit. Mains goes to the user outlets only.** | Only low voltage runs through the panels and roof. A later 120 V US version changes only the supply, inlet and outlets. | REQ-ELE-01, REQ-ELE-03, REQ-AIR-04, REQ-AIR-06; plan VT06, VT07, VT08; ISS-039, ISS-061, ISS-062 |
| D-11 | 2026-10-31 | **Reserve hooks for the voice cockpit**, built later on the in-house Lamp electronics (mic array, speakers, computer), plus a screen and a status light: 12 V 5 A, data, mounts and a cable path. | Spaceship should feel like a cockpit you run by voice. The kit can bolt in later without a redesign (PLAN.md opening). | REQ-VCK-01…06; ELECTRICAL, WALL, CEILING, FRONT DT; IF-15, IF-16; ISS-051, ISS-060 |
| D-12 | 2026-10-31 | **Decor in Dark Yellow** (ACP EV3038). | It matches the plan's material list (VT01) and v1's product photos. The black in the CAD renders and the plan's pictures isn't the product. | REQ-LOOK-03; FRONT decor; ISS-031 |
| D-13 | 2026-10-31 | **STEP files may stay in cm when the file declares it. Drawings and DXF are in mm.** | A STEP file carries its unit and readers convert it, while the shop works from drawings and DXF, which must be in mm (guideline §3.1). Check the unit and a known size after every import or export. | `conventions.md` §2; Phase 0 STEP export; ISS-066 (`AGENTS.md` still says "mm everywhere") |
| D-14 | 2026-10-31 | **US rules come later, but aren't designed out.** | The prototype focuses on structure. The roof, threshold, door width and door pull keep the US path open (PLAN.md). | REQ-SCP-02, REQ-US-01…04; ISS-052, ISS-053 |
| D-15 | 2026-10-31 | **The working name is Spaceship.** Folder names don't change. | The pod looks like a spaceship, and inside it should feel like a cockpit (PLAN.md opening). | All new v2 text |
| D-16 | 2026-10-31 | **Aluminium frames, on the final stack**: 20 × 40 aluminium tube at 400 mm centres for the walls, 20 × 100 for the ceiling. The decor core frames are aluminium too, sized by the door-load check (ISS-045). | Aluminium is lighter than the plan's steel (PLAN.md: about 40 kg). The final stack is the quietest build inside the 400 kg cap: walls 81 mm at 13.1 kg/m², ceiling 141 mm at 17.4 kg/m², pod about 385 kg at 24.2 dB calculated (Hardware Engineer's sound and air check). | BP-21; plan VT10; WALL, CEILING, FRONT; REQ-BLD-01, REQ-BLD-02; ISS-034, ISS-041, ISS-045 |
| D-17 | 2026-10-31 | **No plywood behind the ACP.** The sound mass goes in the floating steel-and-felt lining cassette: a 0.5 mm galvanised steel tray with 25 mm returns, a 25 mm air gap and 9 mm PET felt on its face. | The floating cassette gives more dB per kg than plywood bonded to the skin. It never touches the frame through a screw, and the steel is formaldehyde-free and fire class A1 (Hardware Engineer's sound and air check). | BP-07, BP-12; plan VT02 dropped; WALL, CEILING, LINING; REQ-BLD-01, REQ-BLD-03; ISS-015, ISS-016, ISS-042, ISS-058 |
| D-18 | 2026-10-31 | **Thin floor inside the aluminium base band, with a 20 mm threshold at the door.** | It keeps v1's base band look outside, and gives a low, fixed step at the door. | BASE; REQ-BLD-04, REQ-US-02; ISS-040, ISS-053 |
| D-19 | 2026-10-31 | **The door is left-hinged, as in v1.** The hinge pocket sits in the left decor block (DL). | The fixed panel and the wide faceted right block sit on the right, so the hinges can only go on the left without changing the look (open-questions.md Q3). | FRONT 360000, DL; REQ-BLD-05; ISS-045, ISS-055 |
| D-20 | 2026-10-31 | **Lights: one dimmable, low-heat LED ceiling light plus a soft front face light for video calls, all on 12 V.** The Hardware Engineer picks the parts. | Heat is the users' top complaint and video calls need light on the face (market study). 12 V follows D-10. | ELECTRICAL, CEILING, FRONT DT; REQ-AIR-06; ISS-004, ISS-061 |
