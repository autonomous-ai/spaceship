# Pod market study

Date: 2026-10-31. This is desk research from makers' spec sheets, installation guides, dealer listings and public user comments. Numbers come from makers unless marked "3P" (third party). "nf" means not found. Footprint weight is total weight divided by floor area.

## Summary

1. **FocusPod v1 is already light for its size.** At about 550 kg it comes to 129 kg/m² of floor. The closest rated pods of similar size run 99–136 kg/m²: Orangebox Air3 AIR-23 at 461 kg and Spacestor Portals Studio at 547 kg.
2. **300 kg would be about 40% lighter than any acoustically rated pod of this size.** The only enclosed pod that light is Mamava XL at 318 kg. It uses foam-core aluminium composite panels on an aluminium-frame base, but it has no acoustic rating. Reaching 300 kg needs a different wall build and a glass budget, not just thinner materials.
3. **Nobody ships on 48 × 40 pallets.** Every crate we found is 2.2–2.4 m long, because the panels are full height. No maker claims every part is 20 kg or less. Doing both would be a first.
4. **Users' top complaint is heat and stale air, by far.** Next come sound leaking, then seats and light for video calls. Fan noise and assembly almost never come up.
5. **Pods stay quiet with seals and double skins, not mass.** Glass is the heavy, weak spot. Floorless pods post the weakest sound numbers.

## What customers say

From Hacker News, coworking forums, Trustpilot, workplace press and reviews. Online user reviews are thin, and several sources are vendor-hosted.

1. **Heat and stale air (8+ sources).** "Hot and suffocating after 1 hr." Too hot within 15 minutes from the lights alone. Room's first prototype was nicknamed "the sweatbox". A Framery reached about 2,700 ppm CO2 after someone unplugged the fan.
2. **Not enough booths, and long stays.** Back-to-back calls, booths booked all day, a 32-minute average stay with sessions over 3 hours.
3. **Sound leaks.** "Muffled voices can be heard." Most buyers accept muffled speech rather than silence.
4. **Seats, desk height and light for video.** "Don't scrimp on the seats." The light "casts like the worst shadows."
5. **Feels exposed or boxed in.** "Like being in an aquarium."
6. **Fumes.** In 2019 WeWork pulled thousands of booths over formaldehyde after users reported stinging eyes and headaches.
7. **Delivery waits** and refunds, in the few delivery reviews there are.

Rarely mentioned: fan noise (a soft hum is fine; a dead fan is not), assembly and shipping damage.

People love: real privacy ("I heard nothing"), fast setup with no building work, and fresh air done right ("90-minute calls without opening the door").

## What we learned, and what changes in v2

| Lesson | Change to the v2 plan |
|---|---|
| 300 kg is beyond anything rated on the market | Keep the target. Study sandwich walls (foam-core or honeycomb composite with the same yellow ACP face, on aluminium frames) next to the plan's frame + ACP + plywood. Ask the team to build one wall sample and the door early and test them for sound. |
| Glass is about a third of a 300 kg budget and the weak spot for sound | Keep the glass layout (the look). Compare 8 mm tempered with 4+4 laminated acoustic glass, which weighs about the same and is quieter. Seal the glass in profiles. |
| Seals beat mass; floorless pods leak most | Gasket on every panel joint, a drop seal on the door, absorbent fill in the cavities. A thin floor with a base seal, not no floor. |
| Fresh air is the top complaint | Air becomes a full requirement: CO2 at or under 1,000 ppm for 2 people over 2+ hours. That means at least 20 l/s, aiming for about 70 l/s (a full air change every 2 minutes). Fans start by themselves when someone is inside, have speed control and boost, are wired in so nobody unplugs them, and get silenced vents. Keep light heat low. |
| Nobody ships on 48 × 40, and no kit keeps every part at 20 kg or less | Keep both targets. Split walls into bands about 1.2 m long (Air3 stacks 5 bands) so they stand on 48 × 40 pallets, unless the study shows a standard long pallet is better overall. |
| 2 people in a few hours is only proven on small booths | Joints that find their own place, no heavy lifting overhead, leveling feet (doors fail on uneven floors), and every part labeled. |
| Fumes ended a whole booth line | Low-emission certified felt, boards and glue from the prototype on. |
| Empty pods leave buyers with the comfort complaints | The pod still ships empty. Add a soft front light for video calls, and later a recommended furniture list. |
| US rules come later, but they shape the roof and the door | Don't design them out. The roof should be able to take a sprinkler head or open on alarm (as Air3's does). Keep the threshold low, the door wide and the door pull light. |
| Makers quote sound four different ways | Test v1 and v2 in the same lab to ISO 23351-1 before claiming "as quiet as v1". |
