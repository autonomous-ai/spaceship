# Test plan: what gets measured, by whom, and what passes

| Doc code | Revision | Status | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| V2-SMP-WD (proposed) | P01 | DRAFT | 2026-10-31 |

Every test stays PLANNED until it has real data (guideline §10). A test on HOLD waits for the item in [hold-list.md](hold-list.md). Items S1 to S7 and rigs R1 to R4 are in [build-list.md](build-list.md).

Each pass or fail limit comes from a requirement or a decision. Where the plan had to choose a number with no source (a sample size, a hold time, a margin), the number is marked assumption, so the PM can change it before testing.

**PM ruling, 2026-10-31 (DECIDED):** the assumed limits are confirmed as written: the 0.5 dB switch rule (T02), torque at most half the lowest spin-out torque (T06), 2000 mm head clearance after a drop (T08), cohesive failure (the bond breaks inside the adhesive, not at a surface) as the bond pass (T09), and the sample sizes and hold times. T01 and T02 on the 1.71 m² panel are screening tests that compare fill and felt; bands at or below the lab's flanking limit are indicative only. The decision-grade sound result is the whole prototype pod tested to ISO 23351-1. T04, T05 and T12 follow D-60's air layout, the air column and the soffit inside the pod, built to the Air Engineer's sizes (HW-AA section 7e, PR #26).

## Order on S1 and S2

T10 runs during the build, while the parts are still separate. T11 runs next, at the factory. T09's flatness record is made before S1 ships to the lab, and then T01 and T02 run at the lab. T09's cut-open check comes last, because it destroys the skin bond.

## Rules for every result

- **What a PASS needs** (guideline §10; conventions §9): the method and standard, the conditions, the item and its drawing revision, the instrument with its serial and calibration date, the result, and who (by role) and when. Without all of them, the test stays NOT TESTED.
- **Photos** of every item and every set-up, filed with the result.
- **No names:** the repo is public, so results name roles and accredited labs by accreditation only, with no personal names and no quotes (REQ-DOC-03).
- **Labs:** the PM picks the labs. Agents don't contact labs or suppliers (REQ-DOC-02).
- **Feeding results back:** the Hardware Engineer reruns the sound and air model (T01 to T04). The Layout Engineer reruns the layout study (T10, T11). The PM logs each decision the results trigger: fill (T02), torque (T06), felt (T03), cartridge split (T12), weight trims (T10).
