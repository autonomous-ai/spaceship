# WALL: the ACP skin's free spans

Generated by `make_acp_spans.py` from the build formulas. The skin is bonded to every frame member, so its free spans are the bays between members (the fill bays). Deflection: the bay as a plate simply supported on 4 edges (on the safe side), 3 mm ACP with 0.21 mm aluminium skins (HW-SEL A1), D = 64205 N·mm. Loads are reference cases (assumption), not a pass mark.

**Pass mark: the ACP maker's span limit — HOLD (not in the repo; Tien Anh warned that the ACP flexes without plywood).**
