# FRONT decor blocks: the ACP skin's free spans (D-131)

Generated by `skin_spans.py` (plate solvers in `skin_plates.py`) from the blocks' STEPs as built (v2/03-Manufacturing/front). D-130 changed only the wall of DR's frame tubes, not where they sit (checked against the code), so DR's bays stand. The limit (PM ruling 2026-11-01, D-131): **2 mm or less under a 100 N push at a bay's centre, edges simply supported**. The skin: 3 mm ACP with 0.21 mm aluminium skins, D = 64205 N·mm, as WALL's check. The tubes are taken as rigid, as WALL's check takes them. Grid 2 mm.

A bay is a part of a skin face that no tube touches. Its edges: a tube's bonded strip (simply supported; the second figure takes the bond as holding the edge flat, clamped), a fold to the next face (an edge when the faces turn 10° or more; see the fold check), or a cut edge (free: pockets, slots, open joints). The load goes at the bay's centroid and at its deepest point, the larger counting.
