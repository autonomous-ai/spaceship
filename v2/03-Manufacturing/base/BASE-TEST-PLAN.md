# BASE sample tests

| Doc code | Revision | Status | Date | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V2-BASE-TP | P01 | PLANNED | 2026-11-01 | BASE Engineer |

Physical tests for the BASE parts of the factory sample. Every test is **PLANNED / NOT TESTED** until the factory records real data (guideline §10). The pass marks are assumptions until the PM confirms them. The wall-and-door sample package (`v2/06-Samples/wall-door-sample/test-plan.md`) runs its own tests; these add what BASE needs.

| ID | What | Sample | Method | Pass mark | Feeds | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BT-01 | Floor panel edge load | F1 bonded to its 3 mm foam (110401), on a flat concrete floor | 1000 N through a Ø25 pad, 20 mm from (a) a straight panel edge and (b) the half-lap seam with F2's lap on it; dial gauge on the panel top next to the pad | Deflection 1.0 mm or less under load; no crack or lasting dent over 0.2 mm after unloading (assumption) | D-76, D-85 (poplar-core ply only if it passes) | PLANNED |
| BT-02 | Chair castor over the edges | As BT-01 | An office chair castor (Ø50, twin wheel) loaded to 1000 N, rolled 100 times across a panel edge and across the seam | No step over 1 mm at the seam under the castor; face veneer not torn; seam still flush after the test (assumption) | D-76, D-85 | PLANNED |
| BT-03 | Rivnut in the 1.5 mm band | A 300 mm piece of the 100 × 40 × 1.5 band, 3 rivnuts CAH3-610-4.2 set through 1.5 mm stop washers | Torque to spin-out, and axial pull-out, on each rivnut | Records the values that set the rivnut torque (HOLD-01); added to the joints rivnut test T06 as its 1.5 mm coupon (PM ruling) | J-BASE-01, HOLD-01 | PLANNED |
| BT-04 | Glide on its doubler | A band piece with one glide, its M10 rivnut and the 3 mm doubler | 1500 N (three times the ~500 N glide load) on the glide for 1 minute | Bottom face set under 0.1 mm after unloading; rivnut doesn't turn (assumption) | FD-01 | PLANNED |
| BT-05 | Band levelling | The band pieces of the sample on their glides on the factory floor | Set the glides from above with the 5 mm key; measure the band top at each glide | Band top level within ±1 mm over the sample, in 10 minutes or less (layout study t_level_min) | D-87 (glides preset), layout study | PLANNED |
| BT-06 | Floor-edge seal | F1 against a band piece with the 118001 seal on the band's inner face | Close the 4 mm gap; check contact along the whole edge with a 0.1 mm feeler | Continuous contact, seal not pinched over 75% (assumption until the seal is named, HOLD) | PM ruling (seal at the floor edge) | PLANNED |
